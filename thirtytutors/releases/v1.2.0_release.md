# v1.2.0

*Release date: September 13, 2026*

---

## Fixes

- Fixed a bug where a session time-limit switch (or a dropped connection) could silently lose whatever you were saying at that exact moment, forcing you to repeat yourself. Reconnects now happen in place without ever closing your connection to the app, and any audio that was mid-turn gets replayed automatically.
- Fixed the tutor sometimes forgetting or re-teaching vocabulary from earlier sessions. The tutor now keeps an explicit, growing list of everything it's taught you.
- Fixed an intermittent beep/chirp that could happen during the tutor's speech. Audio chunks are now faded through silence at their boundaries instead of stitched together at full volume.
- Fixed a bug where closing the app could occasionally wipe your profile stats (hours studied, day streak, milestones) completely, rather than just missing the very latest update.
- Fixed the tutor sometimes repeating its last response after a brief connection drop mid-turn.
- Fixed the app not always finishing saving its memory of your most recent exchange if you closed it right after a study session - closing now waits for that to finish first.
- Improved reconnect handling so the tutor is less likely to lose track of what was just said right after a dropped connection.
- Fixed the avatar's fullscreen view starting on a full-body camera angle instead of upper-body.

## Changed

- Removed the per-batch cap on how much vocabulary gets captured during summarization, so a rich lesson isn't trimmed down before it's even saved.
- Forced mic calibration on new profile creation.
- Forced app\assets update on app launch.
- Removed the second backup model, now the app uses only gemini 3.1

## Added

- Connection reconnects now appear in Langfuse (as `session_connect`) when tracing is enabled, showing the exact system instructions sent to the tutor - including the injected memory summary, taught vocabulary, and review terms - for the initial connect and every later reconnect.
- Background images for each of the 30 tutors.
- Short video tutorials on the Get Started page showing how to get a free Gemini API key, how to create a new session, and how to setup hands-free mode.
